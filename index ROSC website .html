THE ROSC PERSPECTIVE

Healthcare workforce challenges deserve continuous attention.

Annual engagement surveys offer a snapshot. ROSC is being built to help organizations listen more frequently, understand unit-level experiences, and make workforce feedback more actionable.

A DIFFERENT KIND OF MEASUREMENT

Two views. A clearer picture.

Our dual-layer scoring concept separates short-term shifts in sentiment from broader workforce conditions.

01 / REAL-TIME SIGNAL

ROSC Pulse

Brief check-ins designed to monitor changing workforce sentiment between comprehensive assessments and help surface emerging concerns.

A view of what may be happening now ↗
02 / COMPREHENSIVE VIEW

ROSC Aggregate Score

A broader measure designed to help leaders understand unit-level workforce conditions and follow changes across assessment periods.

A view of broader workforce conditions ↗
